# weapons.csv

The weapon classes each game's weapons page names: 12 in MH3U, 14 in MH4U and MHGU (Charge Blade and Insect Glaive added).

| Column | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| game | MH3U, MH4U or MHGU | `source` |
| weapon_class | the class's name | `source` |
| source | the game's weapons page | — |

Underwater movesets: the 12 MH3U classes are the ones that can have MH3U underwater motions to extract. Charge Blade and Insect Glaive have none in any game, so theirs would be authored (docs/sources/authored-register.md). Which 3U classes have full underwater movesets is to be confirmed from MH3U's files (README.md).
